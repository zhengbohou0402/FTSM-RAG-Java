UKM Registration Guide

1. Online Registration
Upload all available documents to the JoinUKM system. This may include the tuition payment receipt, Eval, EMGS documents, and other materials currently available.

2. Medical Examination Process

Option A: UKM Health Centre
1. Print and complete the Report of Health Examination Form in advance.
2. Go to the UKM Health Centre.
3. Go to Window 19.
4. Submit the form and pay the RM250 medical examination fee.
5. Complete checks at A3, A2, and A1.
6. Return to A3 and wait for the medical report.
7. Return to Window 19 and submit the report.
8. Wait for the doctor to stamp and sign.
9. Go to the photocopy room on the second floor of the Health Centre. Photocopying costs about RM5 and may require cash.
10. Submit the original and photocopy at Window 19.
11. Scan the medical report, including the original and the first page of the photocopy, and upload it to the JoinUKM system.

Medical examination reminder:
Blood and urine tests are required. Drink enough water before the test.

Option B: External Designated Hospital
1. Complete the medical examination at an external designated hospital.
2. Obtain the medical report.
3. Bring the X-ray film and report to the UKM Health Centre.
4. Take a queue number and wait for the doctor to stamp and sign.
5. Go to the photocopy room on the second floor of the Health Centre. Photocopying costs about RM5 and may require cash.
6. Scan the medical report, including the original and the first page of the photocopy, and upload it to the JoinUKM system.

3. Certification and Visa Endorsement
After completing the medical examination:
1. Go to UKMShape.
2. Verify whether all JoinUKM documents have been uploaded completely.
3. Verify tuition fee, personal bond, and visa fee status.
4. Verify academic qualifications. Bring originals and photocopies of graduation certificate, degree certificate, and notarized documents.
5. Complete visa endorsement. First scan the QR code and fill in the information. Then submit the medical report, Eval, Evisa, and passport.

4. Passport Collection
On the second day after EMGS reaches 100%, bring the temporary passport to PKP to collect the passport. PKP is located on Level 4 of Pusanika.

Registration reminders:
Bring as many original documents and photocopies as possible for offline registration. Bring a laptop to handle unexpected document or system issues.
Offline payment may only support cash, Visa, or Mastercard. UnionPay may not be supported.

UKM Student Pass Renewal Guide

Required documents may include:
1. Attendance report.
2. Grade report.
3. Passport scan, including cover.
4. Renewal application form.
5. Student financial system screenshot.
6. Student confirmation letter.
7. Visa processing fee.
8. EMGS fee.

Renewal steps:
1. Fill in the renewal Google Form and wait for email notification.
2. When EMGS reaches 50%, go to PKP and submit the passport.
3. On the second day after EMGS reaches 100%, bring the temporary passport to PKP to collect the passport.

Renewal application form and renewal Google Form:
https://www.ukm.my/ukmshape/renewal--application/
