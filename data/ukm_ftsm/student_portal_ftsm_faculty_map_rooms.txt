FTSM Faculty Map Rooms and Facilities
====================================

Source type: Student portal / FTSM faculty map room list
Primary source: FTSM faculty map UI and provided map screenshot
Audience: FTSM students and visitors looking for rooms, labs, offices, lecture rooms, tutorial rooms, and facilities by building block.
Note: This is a location guide for navigation. For formal office changes, room availability, or access rules, verify with FTSM or the relevant office.

Overview
--------
The FTSM faculty map groups rooms and facilities by building blocks A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H. Students can use the block name and level to locate lecture rooms, tutorial rooms, labs, offices, common rooms, and service areas.
